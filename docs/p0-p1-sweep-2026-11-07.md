(`8938fe8ce`, [docs/p0-p1-sweep-2026-09-10.md](p0-p1-sweep-2026-09-10.md)) up to

## Known and NOT fixed

- **Dark-mode residue on the X4/X3.** SDK `69349ec` (taken from upstream:
  "Remove dark background hint feature") dropped `writeRamInverted` and the
  dark-background branch from `Ssd1677Driver`, `Uc8253X3Driver`, `Uc8279*` and
  `Uc8179`, so with inverted content a FAST no longer re-drives unchanged
  pixels and light residue can build between full refreshes. Deliberate
  upstream; a silent visual change for dark-mode readers. Owner's call.
- **Theoretical touch wedge** (`InputManager.cpp:1119-1128`): a TMA525C report
  claiming more than 128 bytes is not drained, INT stays low, and the 3 s stale
  release (which only runs with INT high) never fires. Real reports are at most
  107 bytes; not reachable on this controller.
- **Update Firmware leaves the radio up after it exits** (no `onExit` →
  `SavedWifiJoin::release()`), holding off main-loop light sleep until the next
  deep sleep. Recorded as deliberate in `docs/saved-wifi-join-2026-10-20.md`
  item 4.
- **"Albo" in `src/FontDisplayNames.h`** (2bcb4d1f6, 2026-09-14 ruling) predates
  the 2026-10-11 rule that nothing of Albo goes into the public forks. Policy
  question for the owner, not a bug.
- **Picco: a finger resting on the glass** keeps the loop at 10 ms polling and
  holds off prewarm/prerender. Battery and speed only.
- **`/claude-key.txt` and `/github-token.txt` are served by `/download` and
  WebDAV GET** — an accepted risk by owner ruling (`TODO.md:1692-1711`), not
  re-reported.

## Checked and found NOT a bug

- **Picco deep-sleep order** (`main.cpp:792`, `Storage.prepareForDeepSleep()`
  just before `startDeepSleep`): `activityManager.goToSleep()` runs the reader's
  `onExit` (which suspends and commits any section build) and the sleep frame is
  written and closed before it, so no write handle is open when the card is
  prepared.
