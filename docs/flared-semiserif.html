type-coverage.html sorts the 37 curated families into 22 structural classes and finds two of them empty. Typewriter was answered by IBM Plex. This is the other: the glyphic-incised class, where the stem widens into its terminal instead of ending in a bracketed serif — serif-like weight distribution, no actual serifs. The canonical text face is Optima.

Optima is not on this page, and cannot be. It is Monotype's, there is no libre cut, and nothing here is a metric clone of it. Every face below is an OFL face that reaches for the same idea from its own direction. If the answer to this cell turns out to be "buy Optima", that is a legitimate outcome of the bench — three of the catalogue's families are already commercial or webfont cuts that build from local_fonts/.

A browser at 2x cannot answer the question this cell actually poses. Every specimen below is anti-aliased on a high-density display. The device renders 1-bit at an x-height of 12–18 px, and the faces that died in the previous two benches died exactly there — Archivo's s sealed its apertures shut at 11 px until the auto-hinter was forced on. A flared stem is a stem that gets thinner before it flares, which is the worst possible shape to threshold. Expect this page to flatter every candidate.

Specimens, matched x-height

Set so every face shows the same x-height, which is how sans-candidates.yaml and grotesque-candidates.yaml were judged — comparing at equal point size compares nothing, because a 0.42 and a 0.65 x-height at 16 pt are different sizes of type. The four targets are the reader's own slots. Colour, set width and how far the flare survives shrinking are the things to look at.

What the bench still has to decide

Nothing on this page is evidence about the device. If a face survives triage it goes into a flared-candidates.yaml sibling of the other two and gets built, and only then can these be answered:

  • Does the flare survive 1-bit rendering at 12 px x-height, or does it threshold away into a plain sans — leaving a face that costs a slot and fills no cell?
  • Do the slots land? Every curated family hits x-height 12/14/16/18 px on the hinted render. A face whose hinting skips a pixel value cannot hold a slot, which is what forced force_autohint: true on Archivo.
  • Set width against Libre Franklin at matched x-height. A small-x-height face like Forum has to be set larger to match, and pays for it in characters per line.
  • Latin Extended-A coverage, and whether a real italic exists or only a slanted roman.
