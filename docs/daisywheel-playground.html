Daisywheel — vertical-column picks

Every petal's letters read as a vertical column, everywhere on the disc — ↑ (side Up) always takes the top letter, ⏎ (Select) the middle, ↓ (side Down) the bottom, at every wedge angle. No icons on the wheel. Hold a pick for uppercase — long-press means nothing else. ←→ (front Left/Right) rotate, Esc is Back. Two rings: ABC ⇄ 123 — numbers and symbols consolidated into one ring — and flipping stays on the same petal: press ⇄ on the utility petal, land on the other ring's utility petal.
