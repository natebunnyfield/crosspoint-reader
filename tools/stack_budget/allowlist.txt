# Format, one frame per line:

#   <budget-bytes> <repo-relative-file> <demangled signature exactly as -fstack-usage prints it>  # reason

# The reason after '#' is MANDATORY. An entry without one is rejected and the
# gate fails -- an exemption nobody had to justify is how a budget dies.

# ratcheting is just "re-run, then lower the numbers the tool reports as slack".
